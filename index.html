3 FAQs
What happens after I submit my listing?
Your request is immediately logged in our admin Owner List. An assigned specialist will call you to verify specs, inspect the property, and match it with corporate buyers.
Are there upfront marketing fees?
No upfront fees whatsoever. Listing your warehouse, factory, or land on MyFactorySpace is 100% free until a tenancy or sale agreement is successfully executed.

Yes. We evaluate floor loading specs (ton/m²), electrical substations, ceiling height clearances, and DOE zoning approvals to maximize your asking value.
